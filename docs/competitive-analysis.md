# RememberMe — Competitive & IP Research

> **Living document.** Maintained by Olli. Updated as new research lands and as launch-plan decisions are made.
> Last updated: 2026-09-21


**Date:** 2026-09-21 | **Research basis:** web index + one live browser verification of remento.co
**RememberMe spec (founder's):** voice-first collaborative family storytelling; storyteller records via text/email link with no app install and no login; verbatim transcription ("we don't invent what they might have said"); story-level permissions; provenance chain (who said it, when, original audio preserved); "Family Archivist" role; NO AI avatar of deceased persons in MVP; tiers under consideration: free / ~$99 legacy / ~$179–199 family / hardcover book upsell.

---

## 1. Remento Product Deep-Dive (remento.co)

Remento is the direct incumbent competitor and the closest overlap with the RememberMe spec. (Key facts below were **verified live** on remento.co on 2026-09-21; index-sourced items are marked.)

**Company:** Heirloom Media Technologies Inc. Founded by Charlie Greene after recording oral-history interviews with his mother following her 2017 cancer diagnosis. Shark Tank deal with Mark Cuban. Claims 1M+ stories recorded and 4.9/5 on Trustpilot (index: [pulse2.com](https://pulse2.com/storytelling-app-company-remento-closes-3-million/), [shoutoutla.com](https://shoutoutla.com/meet-charlie-greene-co-founder-ceo-of-remento/)).

**End-to-end flow (verified live):**
1. Buyer picks prompts (hundreds, curated) or writes their own, or uploads photos to spark memories.
2. Each week the storyteller gets a prompt by email or text. Recording requires **no app, no login, no password — "two clicks and they're recording on any device"** (audio or video, their choice).
3. **Speech-to-Story™** turns each recording into a written chapter. Critically, it is *not* verbatim-by-default: the user chooses between a **"word-for-word transcript" or "flowing narrative"**, can switch first/third person, adjust length, and edit freely. "Speech-to-Story™ turns each recording into a polished written chapter."
4. Stories are shared instantly with family; reactions/photos can be added.
5. Output: full-color 8"×10" hardcover book (up to 380 pages), one included per $99 purchase, with **a QR code on every page that plays the original recording**. Original recordings are always preserved and downloadable.

**Pricing (verified live):** $99 = one year of unlimited story prompts for one storyteller + one book credit + unlimited collaborators; free US shipping; 30-day money-back guarantee. Extra copies $69 each. Optional renewal $99/year or $12/month. If you don't renew you keep and can download everything ("your stories are yours forever"). E-book reportedly $49.99 extra (index: [Storyworth FAQ](https://welcome.storyworth.com/blog/remento-faq---10-things-to-know-before-you-buy)).

**Positioning:** "A keepsake book that lets you hear their voice forever." Voice-first, zero-friction for seniors, gift-driven (birthday/Father's Day/Mother's Day). Publishes comparison pages against Storyworth, My Life In A Book, and Storykeeper.

**What overlaps with RememberMe:** voice-first capture; no-login text-link recording; weekly prompts; $99 anchor price; original audio preserved via QR codes; family collaboration; hardcover book as the anchor artifact. **This is the competitor to differentiate from most aggressively.**

**Where RememberMe's spec already differs:** (a) verbatim-by-default transcription policy vs. Remento's AI-polished/re-written prose default; (b) story-level permissions and a provenance chain (Remento markets none of this — stories are shared family-wide instantly); (c) a "Family Archivist" identity; (d) RememberMe has no explicit statement on written input — Remento offers *no* writing option (voice/video only per [Storyworth's review](https://welcome.storyworth.com/blog/remento---how-it-works-pricing-reviews-top-alternatives)).

## 2. "Momento" Profile — name ambiguity, most likely candidate = Meminto Stories

**There is no major company named exactly "Momento" in the family-storytelling/memoir space.** After multiple targeted searches, the most likely intended referent is **Meminto Stories (meminto.com)** — a phonetic near-match operating in the identical product category. Secondary possibilities: Memento (memento.com, group-video gifts) and Life's Moments App. A video game called "Momento" and a hobby GitHub project exist but are irrelevant.

**Meminto Stories (meminto.com)** — German AI-assisted biography platform, 4.7/5 customer rating (index: [globenewswire.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/14/3218831/0/en/Meminto-Stories-Unveils-Version-5-1-with-Enhanced-Features.html)):
- **How it works:** 52 curated questions about your life, delivered weekly by email or answered all at once. Spoken memories are transcribed via "StoryScribe" speech-to-text, and "Meminto AI" optimizes/polishes the text. Up to 250 photos plus audio and video can be uploaded. Answers become book chapters.
- **Output:** full-color 6×9" hardcover book, up to 300 pages; "starting at US$99, additional copies from US$39"; tiered by page count ($99 up to 100 pages, $149 up to 300 pages per [Storyworth's review](https://welcome.storyworth.com/blog/meminto-stories-reviews-pricing-alternatives)); ships in ~8–12 days; worldwide shipping.
- **Collaboration:** "Assistant Mode" for co-creation with family; invite family/friends by email or text to contribute stories and photos. Themed books (childhood, travel, wedding, military service) and **memorial books** (post-mortem, answered by family/friends).
- **Positioning:** senior-friendly, guided, "no typing needed," gift-oriented. v5.1 (Jan 2026) added direct preview editing, smart media library, one-click archive export.
- **Vs. RememberMe:** same $99 anchor and weekly-prompt model, but Meminto is writing/transcription-plus-AI-polish with photo-first book design, and its storyteller works inside the platform/app rather than via a zero-login link. No story-level permissions or provenance marketing.

**Memento (memento.com)** — group-video gift service: invite contributors via email/phone or a shareable link; they record video clips on any device; organizer assembles a video up to 60 minutes. No book, no memoir product. ([memento.com/how-it-works](https://memento.com/how-it-works))

## 3. "Storybook" Profile — name ambiguity, most likely candidates

**There is no dominant standalone "Storybook" company in the family-memory space** (and per the mission, Storybook the UI-component library is excluded). Closest literal match:

**StoryTiling's "Storybook" (storytiling.com)** — a product literally named "Storybook": guided process to record a loved one's life stories in an app (30+ Life Chapters; ask questions via the app or interview live); then a human curator organizes the stories into "a beautiful, personalised website" (a digital Storybook) delivered by email link with a private password in ~3 business days; password-protected and shareable; no time limit; launch pricing USD 49 (was 199). Positioning is human-curation rather than AI. ([storytiling.com/shop](https://storytiling.com/storytiling-shop/p/storybook), [gift page](https://storytiling.com/gift-a-virtual-storybook-of-their-life))

**Adjacent "storybook" players the founder may have meant:**
- **Tellioh (tellioh.app):** AI personalized-story platform — turns a real memory into an illustrated, AI-narrated story; life-story keepsakes for grandparents; **cloned family-narrator voices** ("Voice recordings are used only to create your family's private narrator voice"); $9.99/mo Premium; printed hardcover eligibility. This is the one competitor that *does* AI voice cloning of family members — the exact thing RememberMe excludes from its MVP, making it a useful contrast/foil.
- **StoryKeeper (storykeeper.com):** speak/write/audio/video → professionally edited hardcover with **QR codes linking each chapter to the original recording**; 12,000+ books printed; no time limit; unlimited color photos. Near-identical feature set to Remento.
- **"Storybook" kids' bedtime-story apps** (generic): AI-generated personalized bedtime stories for children, incl. Google's Gemini "Storybook" feature (Aug 2025). Not family-history products.
- Also noted in the space: **Storii** (phone-call memoir capture — calls any phone incl. landlines 3×/week, auto-transcribes; [amazon.com](https://www.amazon.com/stores/Storii-RecordyourMemoir/page/B62E02BB-FEF3-4FCE-8260-C0A6DC0A1E72)), **Memorygram** (multi-storyteller books with QR codes to voice; [memorygram.com](https://checkout.memorygram.com/)), **Everwhen** ("Original voice preserved", "Private by default", private family storybook; [everwhen.app](https://www.everwhen.app/en)) — Everwhen's positioning is closest to RememberMe's ethos.

## 4. Storyworth Reference (incumbent)

Founded 2012, San Francisco; self-reports 35M+ stories, 1M+ books, 50,000+ five-star Trustpilot reviews (index: [welcome.storyworth.com](https://welcome.storyworth.com/blog/preserve-grandparents-stories-methods)).
- **Flow:** weekly question by email/text for a year; storyteller replies by typing (email/web) or by phone — "Story Calls" (word-for-word transcription; Storyworth calls the storyteller, works on any phone incl. landline, no app), "Magic Interviews" (guided conversation), "Family Calls". "Magic Questions" generates personalized prompts; built-in proofreader; unlimited color photos.
- **Pricing (2026, index):** Basic $59/yr (writing only + one hardcover); Color $109/yr (adds phone storytelling with transcription + monthly Family Call); Unlimited $199/yr (weekly guided phone interviews). Extra copies $39–$99. Free e-book + audiobook on Color. ([telloom.co](https://www.telloom.co/blog/storyworth-alternatives), [memoirji.com](https://memoirji.com/blog/best-storyworth-alternatives-2026/))
- **Key contrast vs. RememberMe:** write-first DNA; even phone-dictated stories end as plain text — **original audio is not saved or included** (per [Remento's comparison](https://www.remento.co/journal/capture-family-stories-and-memories-top-storyworth-alternatives-for-canada)); no story-level permissions/provenance; editing access never expires.

## 5. IP / Liability Assessment

### 5a. Patents — no blocking patents found
- **No US patents or published applications were found assigned to Remento/Heirloom Media Technologies, Storyworth, or Meminto** covering AI story transcription, photo-prompted storytelling, or keepsake-book generation (Google Patents assignee searches, 2026-09-21). Remento's "Speech-to-Story™" carries a ™ (trademark-style) designation only — no patent signal.
- Older generic publications exist (e.g., US20140237330A1, a memoir-production workflow with transcribed interviews) but are not owned by any of these competitors.
- **Practical read:** the core workflow (record voice → transcribe → print book) is an unpatented product idea. Patent liability risk for launching a voice-first storytelling product appears low. (This is research, not a freedom-to-operate opinion — counsel should run a proper FTO search.)

### 5b. Trademarks — the real risk area, including the RememberMe name
- **REMENTO — LIVE/REGISTERED (the key flag).** USPTO Serial No. 97409220, owner Heirloom Media Technologies Inc., filed 2022-05-13, **registered 2024-06-18**, covering entertainment services and websites featuring non-downloadable digital scrapbooks/interviews/biographies ([furm.com mirror](https://furm.com/trademarks/remento-97409220)). "RememberMe" shares the "remem-" prefix, the memory-storytelling meaning, and the identical product class — a USPTO examining attorney could plausibly cite REMENTO as a likelihood-of-confusion bar, and there is real marketplace confusion risk regardless. **This is the single biggest naming risk found.**
- **STORYWORTH — live.** The "S" logo mark is LIVE/REGISTERED (Serial 99155785, Storyworth Inc.), covering SaaS for creating online books + digital on-demand book printing ([trademarkelite.com](https://www.trademarkelite.com/trademark/trademark-detail/99155785/S)); the STORYWORTH word mark is a 14-year-established brand.
- **MOMENTO — no live mark in relevant classes.** Found filings are dead/abandoned (video-production app, watches/jewelry, environmental controls); "UN MOMENTO" is live only for alcoholic beverages (class 33).
- **STORYBOOK — no live mark surfaced in storytelling-app classes**; crowded descriptive-use field. Needs counsel-run TESS clearance.
- **"REMEMBERME" clearance — not clear, two collisions to check:**
  1. **Capcom's "Remember Me"** video-game trademark (filed 2012-05-22, computer game software, class 9; [eurogamer.net](https://www.eurogamer.net/capcom-registers-trademark-for-remember-me)) — phonetically identical to "RememberMe" and in class 9 (software). Live status unconfirmed in this research.
  2. **REMEMBERME.ME** (Ser. 77715107; "website to enable clients to construct pictorial/verbal memorials for departed loved ones") is **abandoned** — but it shows the exact mark was previously used for an adjacent memorial product ([trademarktok.com](https://www.trademarktok.com/trademark/detail/77715107/REMEMBERMEME)).
  - **Bottom line on the name:** no exact live "REMEMBERME" registration in the storytelling-app class was found, but the name sits between a live phonetic near-match (REMENTO, same class) and an identical game-software mark (Capcom). **Do not launch on this name without a full TESS clearance search and a trademark attorney's opinion.** A distinct name (or a visually/phonetically distinct variant) would materially reduce risk.

### 5c. Litigation — none found
- No lawsuits, oppositions, or cease-and-desist actions found between or among Remento, Storyworth, Meminto, StoryKeeper, Tellioh, or others in this space.
- Adjacent signal: in 2026 ex-NPR host David Greene sued Google over AI voice replication in NotebookLM (voice/likeness misappropriation) — evidence that **voice and likeness rights are the live legal frontier** in this category, which cuts in favor of RememberMe's consent-first, no-AI-avatar, provenance-chain design ([mediagazer.com](https://mediagazer.com/260215/p3)).

**Practical liability picture:** What is genuinely protectable here is narrow — brand names/logos (trademark), the specific book/product design (copyright/trade dress), proprietary code and prompt pipelines (trade secret/copyright). The product *idea* — voice capture → book — is not patented by anyone found and is widely practiced (Storyworth since 2012). The founder's exposure is primarily (1) trademark risk on the "RememberMe" name vs. live REMENTO, and (2) ordinary execution/privacy obligations (recordings of real people: consent, right of publicity, data handling) — not patent infringement.

## 6. Differentiation Analysis

### 6a. Feature-by-feature comparison

| Dimension | RememberMe (spec) | Remento | Meminto ("Momento") | Storyworth | StoryTiling Storybook / Tellioh |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Capture friction | Text/email link, no app, no login | **Same**: no app/login/password, 2-click recording (verified live) | App/web platform + email prompts | Email/web reply; phone on paid tiers (landline OK) | App (StoryTiling); account-based (Tellioh) |
| Voice-first vs photo/write-first | Voice-first | Voice/video-first | Voice-to-text + photo-first book design | Write-first; voice only via paid phone tiers | StoryTiling: voice + human curation; Tellioh: AI-generated stories from memories |
| Verbatim vs AI-rewritten | **Verbatim by principle** ("we don't invent"); AI only polishes/organizes | Choice: word-for-word transcript **or** AI "flowing narrative"; defaults to polished prose | Speech-to-text + "Meminto AI" optimization/polish | Typed text; proofreader only (no AI rewrite); phone = word-for-word transcription | Human-curated (StoryTiling); AI-generated (Tellioh) |
| Permissions / provenance | Story-level permissions; provenance chain (who/when/original audio) | None marketed — stories shared instantly with whole family | None found | None found | None found |
| Original voice preserved | Yes, core artifact | Yes — QR code per page/chapter | Audio/video uploadable; book is text+photos | **No** — dictated stories are text only | StoryTiling: recordings organized on web; Tellioh: cloned narrator voice |
| AI avatar of deceased | Explicitly excluded from MVP | Not offered | Not offered | Not offered | **Tellioh offers voice cloning** |
| Pricing | Free / ~$99 / ~$179–199 / book upsell | **$99/yr** + 1 book; extras $69; renew $99/yr or $12/mo | ~$99 tiered by pages (100–300 pp); extras from $39 | $59 / $109 / $199 tiers | StoryTiling digital Storybook $49; Tellioh $9.99/mo |
| Output | Book upsell (TBD format) | 8×10" hardcover ≤380 pp + QR audio | 6×9" hardcover ≤300 pp | 6×9" hardcover ≤300 pp | Website w/ password (StoryTiling); print eligibility (Tellioh) |
| Collaboration model | "Family Archivist" organizer identity | Unlimited collaborators, instant sharing | Assistant Mode co-creation; memorial books | Family can suggest prompts | — |

**Honest note:** RememberMe's spec currently overlaps Remento *heavily* (voice-first, zero-login link, $99, weekly prompts, QR-coded audio book). "Voice-first" and "no login" are **not** differentiators against Remento — they are table stakes Remento already owns and markets ("No writing. No logins. No downloads."). The spec's genuine deltas are the verbatim-truth principle, story-level permissions/provenance, and the Archivist identity.

### 6b. Proposed differentiation angles (grounded in findings, none currently owned by a competitor)

1. **"Verbatim, or it didn't happen" — the anti-AI-fabrication position.** Every competitor either AI-polishes (Remento's "flowing narrative", Meminto's AI optimization) or AI-generates (Tellioh). None stakes its brand on *verbatim fidelity as the product*. Market signal supports this: a 2026 competitive audit notes "anti-AI" positioning emerging at multiple price tiers (Audiobiography $76, My Stories Matter $450) — i.e., distrust of AI rewriting is a validated, growing wedge ([github.com/paulingalls/legacy](https://github.com/paulingalls/legacy)). RememberMe can own "the memoir app that refuses to put words in their mouth."
2. **Story-level permissions + provenance chain.** No competitor offers per-story audience controls or a who-said-it/when/original-audio chain of custody. This turns a real family problem into a feature: sensitive stories (health, feuds, money, "don't tell the kids yet") currently have nowhere safe to live — Remento blasts every story to the whole family instantly. Market as "the family archive with a lock on every drawer."
3. **The Family Archivist identity.** Competitors sell to "the gift-giver." Naming and designing for the Archivist — a role with tools (prompt scheduling, story triage, permissions, print curation) — creates a power-user persona nobody owns, and a natural expansion path (archivists manage multiple elders; the $179–199 family tier fits here).
4. **"No AI ghosts" pledge as a trust brand.** Tellioh already clones family voices; the category is drifting toward AI avatars of the dead. RememberMe's explicit refusal — consent-first, no avatar in MVP — is a differentiating ethical stance *and* a liability reducer (voice/likeness is the live legal frontier per the 2026 Greene v. Google suit). Few startups get to claim the high ground before the scandal; the window is open now.
5. **Completeness guarantee / anti-abandonment design.** The category's known failure mode is the unfinished book (reviews repeatedly cite parents quitting Storyworth's weekly grind; YC-backed Artifact shut down after 3.5 years). A product promise like "a finished keepsake in 90 days or your money back" — delivered via archivist tooling, flexible (non-weekly) cadence, and done-for-you curation — attacks the outcome competitors sell but often fail to deliver.

## 7. Overall Take

- **Closest competitor by far: Remento.** It already executes ~80% of the RememberMe spec (voice-first, zero-login capture, $99, QR-coded audio book). RememberMe cannot win on "easier voice capture" — it must win on *trust* (verbatim fidelity, permissions, provenance, no-AI-avatar pledge).
- **"Momento" and "Storybook" are ambiguous names** with no dominant referents; the most likely intended competitors are Meminto Stories and StoryTiling's "Storybook" product (profiled above), with Tellioh and StoryKeeper as the sharpest adjacent players. Confirm with the founder which they meant.
- **IP risk is low on patents, real on trademark.** No blocking patents found; the workflow is an unpatented, widely practiced idea. But **"RememberMe" is a risky name**: live REMENTO (Reg. 2024, same class, phonetically close) + Capcom's "Remember Me" game mark. Full clearance + counsel before launch; consider a more distinctive name.
- **The differentiation white space is trust infrastructure, not capture UX.** Verbatim-by-default, story-level permissions, provenance chain, and an explicit no-AI-avatar stance are unowned and directly responsive to where the category is heading (voice cloning, AI fabrication, abandonment).
